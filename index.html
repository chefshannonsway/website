About Chef Shannon

Chef Shannon Bush has been a plant-based chef for over 30 years, working as an Executive Chef, Head Baker, Caterer, and Culinary Instructor. She's currently at work on her third cookbook and having a wonderful time leading vegan tour groups throughout Japan.

She loves demonstrating the many benefits of plant-based eating — and proving that healthy food can be truly delicious.
